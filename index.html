ABOUT SAUNA

Sauna started as a simple idea: bring house and disco to the Bible Belt through the form of a dance party. Ten years later, it's grown into something bigger, a quarterly event series, a zine, a mixtape, and a community anchored in queer joy and ecstatic dance. The party has lived in many forms and many locations over the decade, but its pulse has never changed. We are house, disco, funk, and soul.
